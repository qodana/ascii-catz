## Party Lion Design

### Goal
Add a lion as "the uninvited guest" to the end of `party.txt`.

### Chosen Approach
Insert a new section `~ * ~ THE UNINVITED GUEST ~ * ~` between the after-hours block and the closing banner. The lion is drawn with a full mane, expressive face, and large paws to read as unmistakably lion-shaped among the smaller cat and dog ASCII art.

### Details
- The lion ASCII art is ~15 lines tall, centred in the file.
- Accompanied by three flavour lines: the catz pretend not to notice, the dogs pretend not to exist, and the party goes on.
- Placement: lines 74–98 of `party.txt`.

### Constraints
- No changes to existing sections (DJ, dancefloor, snack bar, band, after hours).
- Closing banner (`STAY WEIRD. STAY FLUFFY. STAY UP.`) remains last.
- Consistent style with the rest of the file's ASCII tone.
